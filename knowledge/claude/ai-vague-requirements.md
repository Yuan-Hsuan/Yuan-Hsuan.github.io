## 1. The pain point: good prompting assumes a complete picture you don't always have（痛點）
[[ai-description]] says "picture the finished thing, then describe it." But often you *can't* —
you don't yet know what you want. The instinct is to write a harder, longer prompt. **That's the
wrong fix.** When you're vague, the problem isn't your wording — you're just not at the
Description stage yet. Writing a sharper prompt for a picture you don't have only makes AI guess
more confidently, and hands you a polished version of the wrong thing.

## 2. The fix: switch mode, don't push harder（換模式，不要更用力）
Vagueness is a signal to change *how* you work with AI, not to try again with more effort. From
[[ai-fluency]]'s three modes: stop asking for a finished product (**Automation**) and start
thinking *with* it (**Augmentation**). Here, AI isn't the executor — it's a mirror that helps you
see your own idea. You find the picture *inside* the conversation, not before it.

## 3. Tactic A — ask for a strawman, then decide by reacting（先要草稿，用「反應」找方向）
You often can't invent a spec from a blank page, but you can *react* to something in front of you
instantly. So don't stare at the void — say: *"I'm not sure what I want yet. Give me 3 very
different versions."* One look and you'll know "this one's right, that one's wrong." Your judgment
fires on **reaction, not imagination.** (This is why [[ai-delegation]]'s note calls underdefined
tasks *good* to hand off — AI fills the blank, you edit toward what you actually meant.)

## 4. Tactic B — metaprompting: let AI interview you（讓 AI 反過來問你）
Flip the direction. Instead of describing, ask: *"I want to do X but my requirements are fuzzy.
Don't build anything yet — first ask me 5 questions that would let you write the spec."* Now AI is
the one dragging the picture out of your head. Answer its questions and the "complete image" you
were missing usually assembles itself — then you're ready to describe.

## 5. The rule of thumb（一句話判斷）
* **Have the picture →** Description. Describe it cleanly, let AI execute (Automation).
* **No picture yet →** don't Describe. Augment first — strawman + metaprompting to *form* the
  picture — then describe it. Vagueness isn't a bad prompt; it's the wrong mode.
