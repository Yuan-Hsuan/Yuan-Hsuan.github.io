## The 3 Faces of Diligence（盡責的三個面向）

Diligence = 為「你跟 AI 一起做出來的東西」**負全責**。不是 AI 的鍋——最後 ship 出去、掛你名字的是你。三個面向剛好蓋住整條線：**怎麼做、怎麼講、怎麼放出去**。

### 1. Creation Diligence — 認真地做（過程要盡責）
Be deliberate about *how* you build with AI: pick the right tool/model for the task, give it good
inputs, and stay critically engaged instead of accepting the first fluent answer.

做的**過程**就要盡責——挑對工具／模型、給好的輸入、對第一個「聽起來很順」的答案保持懷疑，而不是照單全收。這跟 [[ai-discernment]] 是一對：discernment 是事後「看出」問題，creation diligence 是一開始就用心做、**少製造問題**。

### 2. Transparency Diligence — 誠實揭露（對外要盡責）
Be honest about AI's role — disclose how and where AI was involved, matched to what your audience
expects. Ask yourself:
* Who is the audience for your project output?
* What expectations might they have regarding AI disclosure?
* How specifically did AI contribute to different aspects of your work?

交東西前先想清楚：**給誰看、對方對「AI 有沒有參與」有什麼期待、AI 到底在哪些部分幫了你**。該講的講清楚，對得上受眾的期待——不誇大 AI，也不假裝全是自己做的。

### 3. Deployment Diligence — 為產出負責（放出去要盡責）
You own the output. Before it ships or gets used, verify it yourself — and accept that the
consequences are yours, not the model's. "The AI wrote it" is never a defense for a bug, a wrong
fact, or a bad call that went out under your name.

放出去的東西你**負全責**。ship 之前自己驗過（這就接到 [[ai-dev-workflow]] Phase 4 的「自己讀 diff」）；出事不能說「是 AI 寫的」——掛你名字的，責任就是你的。

---

**和其他 D 的關係：** Diligence 是整套 [[ai-fluency]] 的**安全網**——[[ai-discernment]] 在你不熟的領域會失靈，這時就靠 Diligence（查證、揭露、負責）補上。
