A VPN establishes a secure, **encrypted "tunnel"** over a public network, letting your device safely connect to a private network. It does two things:

1. **Masks your real IP address** — traffic is routed through the VPN server.
2. **Encrypts the payload** (e.g. IPsec, OpenVPN) — preventing packet sniffing or interception on untrusted networks like public Wi-Fi.


VPN 在公網上建**加密隧道**，讓裝置安全連進私網。兩個核心作用：(1) 隱藏真實 IP（透過 VPN 伺服器代發）、(2) 對封包高強度加密（IPsec/OpenVPN），防止在公用 WiFi 被攔截竊聽。
